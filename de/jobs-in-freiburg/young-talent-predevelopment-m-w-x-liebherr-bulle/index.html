Are you looking forward to starting your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our internship program to launch your career with us and benefit from our hands-on approach. We are looking for candidates available for 12 months internship as of February 2027. Your Responsibilities

  • You prepare 1D based model of power train components and validation with component test bench results
  • You support the development of 1D based model of power train for vehicle system integration
  • You document the developed library for component modeling
  • You support the development of control strategies for hybrid system boosts
  • You design and dimension the components for combustion engines and hydraulic pumps
  • You support the testing of components and engine
  • You create and present the reports and measurement results Your Profile
  • You have successfully completed studies in mechanical engineering or you are about to graduate
  • You have knowledge of 1D System and thermodynamic analysis tool (AMesim, GT-SUITE)
  • You have knowledge of system engineering: hydraulic, combustion engine, transmission
  • You are ready to work in a team with different departments on both components and vehicle sides
  • You possess good communication skills and are fluently speaking English and French Have we awoken your interest? Then we look forward to receiving your online application. If you have any questions, please contact Lisa SCHNEIDER. One Passion. Many Opportunities. The company ​Liebherr Machines Bulle SA is the centre of excellence for combustion engines (diesel and gas engines) and hydraulic components (axial piston pumps and motors). The company develops and produces various products, which find their use in building construction and civil engineering, mining, agriculture, decentralised energy, machine and plant engineering, as well as aerospace.
